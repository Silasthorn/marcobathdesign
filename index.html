What you're getting

The same honest standard on every job — no tile, no shortcuts, and no guessing about what's included.

100% pure acrylic — no tile, no real grout

Every conversion is done in 100% pure acrylic. No tile, and no real grout to scrub or reseal. On the tile-look designs, the grout lines are simulated grout — molded right into the acrylic itself — so you get the tile look without any real grout. The acrylic is manufactured in the USA, and the fixtures and valves are American-made too.

Virtually maintenance free. The smooth acrylic surface never needs abrasives or harsh chemicals — a quick wipe with mild soap and water is all it takes to keep it looking new.

Standard on every job

Your package includes:

  • Multi-function shower fixture — thermostatic valve included in all of our shower designs
  • Storage your way — a shower caddy, or two corner shelves — your choice of stainless steel or a color matched to your walls
  • Grab bar
  • Foldable bench — showers only (a tub doesn't take one)

And no plastic — ever. The fixtures and the valve are solid metal: brass, aluminum, or stainless steel.

Caddy or corner shelves — your choice, included either way. The corner shelves come in two finishes — your choice of stainless steel or a color matched to your walls.

And if the rain head and handheld wand aren't your style, you're not stuck with them — we also carry several fixed single-shower designs: one clean, steady shower head and nothing you don't need. The choice is yours.

Tubs & walk-in showers, your way

Your choice of shower base: a standard 4–5 inch pan, or a low 2-inch pan for easier step-in access. Tubs come in 15-inch and 19-inch heights.

Real glass, built to last

Glass enclosures are 3/8-inch thick glass — sturdy, clear, and made to feel solid every time you use it. The glass door is always a sliding door, with a towel bar on the inside and a towel bar on the outside. The rollers are concealed, which makes the door easy to clean. Framing is minimal — that means better water retention and a cleaner, more pleasing look. Fixture finishes available in chrome, brushed nickel, and matte black — and no plastic, ever: the fixtures and the valve are solid metal — brass, aluminum, or stainless steel.

New plumbing & backer board, every time

We don't cover up old problems. On every job:

  • Plumbing is replaced — including the P-trap under the drain
  • New backer board goes in (green board / purple board) with a waterproof shield behind the walls
  • Acrylic walls run up to 96 inches high

Benches & grab bars, built strong

The foldable bench — showers only; a tub doesn't take one — is anchored directly into the studs and holds over 300 lbs — teak bench and corner bench options are available too. Grab bars are rated to hold over 800 lbs.

Installed in as little as one day

Most conversions are installed in ONE day when we're replacing fiberglass, acrylic, or Corian. Converting from tile may take two days.

Limited lifetime warranty

Product AND labor, covered for life. It covers our product and our labor completely — the only thing no warranty can cover is deliberate damage to the bathroom itself. That's the warranty on the work — plain and simple.

Found mold or damage behind the walls? We fix it — at no extra cost.

Old tubs and showers hide things. If we open your walls and find mold, we treat it. If we find structural damage behind the bathroom walls, we replace the damaged structure. All of it is included — no extra cost to you, no surprise charges once the job starts.

How much will my conversion cost, exactly?

In some cases, zero interest for up to 18 months is available too. Ask at your visit and we will walk through what fits your project.
Do you remodel the whole bathroom?
No — and that's on purpose. I specialize in the tub and shower space only: conversions and replacements done with pure acrylic. No tile, no full remodels. There's a reason for that focus: the tub and shower space is the most common — and the most serious — part of any bathroom redesign. It's where water does its damage, and it's the part that truly needs experts. A vanity, a toilet, a mirror? Almost anyone can swap those. The wet space is where expertise matters — so it's the one thing we do, and we do it right. It keeps the job fast, clean, and focused on the part of the bathroom you use every day.
Who actually does the work?
Your project is contracted, permitted, and installed by the licensed California contractor I represent — a name you'll recognize the moment you hear it: a trusted brand, known in homes nationwide. I'll give you the company name and license number in writing at your visit so you can verify everything with the state (CSLB) before signing.
Is the consultation really free?
Yes — free, at your home, with no obligation. If it's not a fit, you still keep the design ideas and the numbers.

One honest note: most people who reach out have already spoken with one or two contractors, and they're comparing on price and quality. That's exactly why everything here is priced in the open — so before you call, take a moment with the designs and their prices, and know what your project will cost. If those numbers work for you, call with confidence. And yes — the consultation itself is still completely free.
